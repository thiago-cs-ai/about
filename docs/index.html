Product · Rehab IA Pro

AI for clinical reasoning and treatment planning.

I architected and implemented agentic clinical workflows for Rehab IA Pro, a physiotherapy platform in active clinical use. The system helps physiotherapists assess patients, work through differential diagnoses and plan treatment.

Working with a physiotherapist product owner, I connected consultation history, examination findings and supporting documents with a structured assessment workflow. It tracks open questions and clinician-confirmed findings to help the agent guide what to examine next.

Architecture · Claro

Cloud architecture adopted for a national ML rollout.

Claro appointed me to review and rework the cloud architecture for Beegol, an ML diagnostics platform being deployed across a broadband network serving more than ten million subscribers.

My redesign used Amazon Redshift and fourteen months of sampled telemetry for audits and model maintenance, beyond the original one-week application window. The vendor built to the reviewed design; I subsequently helped supervise and audit the implementation.

In separate work at Claro, I built a production multi-cloud self-service portal, implemented cloud governance with an engineer I coordinated, and restored a federal ministry’s Kubernetes platform after a cyberattack.

Founder · Healthcare software

Healthcare claims, from build to acquisition.

I founded the company and led the design and development of its healthcare claims platform. It reconciled insurer payment records against hospital bills, helping billing teams recover denied, unpaid or underpaid amounts.

It resolved most incompatibilities automatically. For the rest, operators could use a resolved discrepancy as a template for the software to propose further matches, then confirm the results.

Hospitals across most regions of Brazil used it to process claims worth billions of reais. The company was acquired, the buyer’s engineering team took over development, and the platform remains in production under successive owners.
